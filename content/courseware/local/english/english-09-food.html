Apple

苹果 · píngguǒ

apple
/ˈæp.əl/
苹果 — A sweet red or green fruit 🍎
a = /æ/
a·pple
首字母 a 发 /æ/ 音 — 嘴巴张大，短音"啊"
I like apples.
我喜欢苹果。
I eat an apple.
我吃一个苹果。

💡 "An apple a day keeps the doctor away!" 🍏

🍞

Bread

面包 · miànbāo

bread
/brɛd/
面包 — Baked food made from flour 🍞
ea = /ɛ/
br-ea-d
字母组合 ea 发 /ɛ/ 音 — 短音"哎"
Bread is good.
面包很好吃。
I have some bread.
我有一些面包。

💡 Bread is made from flour, water, and yeast.

🧀

Cheese

奶酪 · nǎilào

cheese
/tʃiːz/
奶酪 — Yellow food made from milk 🧀
ch = /tʃ/
ch-ee-se
ch 发 /tʃ/ 音，像中文"吃"的开头
I like cheese.
我喜欢奶酪。
I eat cheese.
我吃奶酪。

💡 Cheese is made from milk! 🥛 Mice love cheese! 🐭

🥚

Egg

鸡蛋 · jīdàn

egg
/ɛɡ/
鸡蛋 — Oval food that comes from chickens 🥚
e = /ɛ/
e-gg
首字母 e 发 /ɛ/ 音，和 apple 的 a 发音不同哦
I have an egg.
我有一个鸡蛋。
The egg is good.
这个鸡蛋很好吃。

💡 Eggs can be boiled, fried, or scrambled! 🍳

🐟

Fish

鱼 · yú

fish
/fɪʃ/
鱼 — An animal that swims in water 🐟
sh = /ʃ/
fi-sh
sh 发 /ʃ/ 音，像"嘘"——让手指放在嘴边！🤫
Fish is good.
鱼很好吃。
I like fish.
我喜欢鱼。

💡 Fish live in oceans, rivers, and lakes! 🌊🐠

🧃🥛🍚

Drinks & Grains

饮料和谷物 · yǐnliào hé gǔwù

juice 🧃
/dʒuːs/
果汁 — Liquid made from fruits
milk 🥛
/mɪlk/
牛奶 — White drink that comes from cows
rice 🍚
/raɪs/
米饭 — Small white grains we eat every day
I like juice. I drink milk. I eat rice.
我喜欢果汁。我喝牛奶。我吃米饭。

💡 Rice is a staple food for many people around the world! 🌏

💧

Water

水 · shuǐ

water 💧
/ˈwɔː.tər/
水 — Clear liquid we drink every day
I drink water.
我喝水。
