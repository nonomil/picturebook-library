🌥️ Dark Clouds

Steve and Alex are playing outside. Then they see dark clouds in the sky. There is a big storm coming!

💨 Wind and Rain

The wind blows hard. The rain comes down. Steve and Alex call their friends to come inside.

⚡ Thunder and Lightning

Thunder booms! Lightning flashes across the sky! Steve helps them get inside quickly.

🏠 Safe Inside

Everyone is safe and dry inside. Their home is warm. They wait for the storm to pass.

☀️ Sun Comes Out

The storm stops. The sun comes out! A beautiful rainbow appears. Then everyone goes outside to play again.
