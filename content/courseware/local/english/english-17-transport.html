🚂 They are ...

Use they for more than one thing.

⛵ Those are ...

⛵⛵ boats

🏁 The Great Transport Race!

Tom drives a car 🚗. Anna rides a bike 🚲.

He goes fast, but she goes faster!

This car is red. Those bikes are blue.

They take the train 🚂. Those boats ⛵ are on the river.

A big plane ✈️ flies in the sky.

Who wins? Everyone wins because we all go together! 🎉
