🏠 Lesson 22: At the Farm ▶

Steve visits a big farm today. He sees many animals and learns about farm life.

"Welcome to Green Valley Farm!" says Farmer Tom. "Let me show you around."

Steve is excited! 🐄

First, Steve sees fluffy sheep. The sheep make soft sounds: "Baa baa!"

"Sheep live in the field," says Farmer Tom. "They eat grass all day."

Steve laughs and pets a baby lamb. 🐑

Next, Steve meets the cow and horse. The cow is black and white. Moooo!

"Cows live in the barn and eat hay," says Tom. "Horses work on the farm."

Steve feeds a carrot to the horse. It neighs happily! 🐴

Steve sees pink pigs rolling in mud. Oink oink! 🐷

"Pigs eat and sleep in the barn," says Tom. "They love to roll in mud to stay cool."

Nearby, chickens peck at seeds. Cluck cluck! 🐔

Farmer Tom takes Steve to the big red barn. Inside it is warm and cozy.

"Many animals live here. Farmers work hard to take care of them."

Steve sees a horse in its stall and chickens on perches. 🐓

Last, Steve visits the big field. Crops grow tall in the warm sun.

"Food doesn't just appear at the shop," says Tom. "It grows right here on the farm!"

Steve picks a ripe tomato. He makes a wish to come back again. 🍅

What a wonderful day at the farm! Steve learned so much. 🎉

Animals live on farms. Farmers work hard. Food grows in fields.

Steve waves goodbye to Farmer Tom and all the animals. See you next time! 👋
