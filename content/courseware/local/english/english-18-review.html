3

Monday Morning ☀️

🏠 Home · School · Morning

On Monday morning, Lily wakes up at home. She is happy! But she feels a little hungry.

"Time for school!" says Mom. Lily walks to the kitchen. She sits down to eat breakfast. Then she stands up and gets her bag.

She walks to school with a big smile. It's going to be a great week!

home school morning happy hungry walk sit stand
4

Tuesday – Park & Shop 🌳

📍 Park · Shop · Afternoon

On Tuesday afternoon, Lily goes to the park. She loves to run and jump on the grass!

"I'm getting tired," she says after playing. So she sits on a bench to rest.

Then Mom takes her to the shop to buy some fruit. Lily picks apples and oranges. What a fun day!

park shop afternoon run jump tired
5

Wednesday – Farm 🐄

🚜 Farm · Time Words · Sight Words

On Wednesday, the class goes to the farm! Today is a special day. Lily sees cows, sheep, and chickens.

"What will we see tomorrow?" she asks. "Maybe we can come back at night to see the stars."

They ride the bus back to school. Lily ate an apple on the bus. She feels so brave after petting a big horse!

farm today tomorrow night bus ate brave
6

Thursday – Zoo 🐯

🐾 Zoo · Train · Feelings

On Thursday, everyone takes the train to the zoo! Lily is a little scared of the lions, but she is excited too.

She sees monkeys jump and birds dance. "I saw a penguin swim!" she shouts. "I did it — I touched a snake!"

When Dad came to pick her up, Lily says, "This was the best day!"

zoo train scared jump dance saw did came
7

Friday – Sports Day 🏅

🏃 Running · Swimming · Winning

On Friday morning, Sports Day begins! Lily ran faster than ever. She can swim so fast in the pool.

"I want to win!" she thinks. She runs and jumps. In the afternoon, the race is over.

Lily ran her best race. She didn't win first place, but she did her best and that made her happy!

morning afternoon ran swim fast win
8

The Weekend 🌈

🚲 Bike · Boat · Plane · Love

On the weekend, Lily's family goes on an adventure! Dad rides his bike. Mom takes the boat across the lake.

Lily looks at the sky and sees a plane flying high. She looks at the water and sees fish.

"I love my family!" Lily says. She rides her bike along the path with the wind in her hair. What a perfect weekend!

bike boat plane sky water ride love
9

The Big Story Complete 🎉

📖 Phase 3 Complete!

You have learned all 42 Phase 3 words! Let's celebrate with the full story together:

On Monday morning, Lily is happy and hungry. She walks to the kitchen, sits down, then stands up for school.

On Tuesday afternoon, she runs and jumps at the park until she is tired. Then she goes to the shop.

On Wednesday she goes to the farm. She ate an apple on the bus. She is brave! Today was fun. What about tomorrow? Maybe night time!

On Thursday she takes the train to the zoo. She is scared but she did it! She saw animals jump and dance. Dad came to get her.

On Friday morning is Sports Day! Lily ran fast. She can swim too. In the afternoon, she tries to win!

On the weekend, Lily rides her bike. She sees a boat and a plane in the sky and fish in the water. She loves her family!

🎊 42 words · 5 sound blocks · Phase 3 complete!
