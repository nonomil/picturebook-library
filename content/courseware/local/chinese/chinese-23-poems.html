📖 第一首
静夜思
唐 · 李白 (Lǐ Bái)
床前明月光 chuáng qián míng yuè guāng
疑是地上霜 yí shì dì shàng shuāng
举头望明月 jǔ tóu wàng míng yuè
低头思故乡 dī tóu sī gù xiāng
💭 Moonlight before my bed — could it be frost? I lift my head to gaze at the moon, then bow my head and think of home.
📖 第二首
春晓
唐 · 孟浩然 (Mèng Hàorán)
春眠不觉晓 chūn mián bù jué xiǎo
处处闻啼鸟 chù chù wén tí niǎo
夜来风雨声 yè lái fēng yǔ shēng
花落知多少 huā luò zhī duō shǎo
💭 Spring dawn — I sleep without noticing morning. Everywhere I hear birds singing. Last night there was wind and rain. I wonder how many petals have fallen.
📖 第三首
登鹳雀楼
唐 · 王之涣 (Wáng Zhīhuàn)
白日依山尽 bái rì yī shān jìn
黄河入海流 huáng hé rù hǎi liú
欲穷千里目 yù qióng qiān lǐ mù
更上一层楼 gèng shàng yī céng lóu
💭 The sun sets behind the mountains, the Yellow River flows to the sea. To see a thousand miles further, climb one more story higher.
